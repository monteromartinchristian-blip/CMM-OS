## 1. Why this remediation exists

The V3 redo correctly closed the caller-metadata authority-forgery class, but
the independent re-audit found that the approved design amendment §10
("the trusted authority channel must preserve source identity and provenance")
was not implemented: `ReasoningAuthorityContext` carried a naked
`authoritative_claim_ids` tuple, so a trusted context could promote a clinical
claim to `CONFIRMED` without carrying any canonical source provenance for the
evidence that established it.

## 2. Remediation scope

Targeted only. The runtime-only trusted channel is kept. No return to metadata
authority. No registry, store, resolver, loader, engine, Health database,
cryptographic signing, or Phase 11 behaviour.

### 2.1 `cmm/cognitive/reasoning_rule_contracts.py` / `cmm/cognitive/__init__.py`

- New shared neutral typed projection `AuthoritativeSourceClaim`
  (`claim_id`, `source_domain`, `purpose`, `provenance`) reusing the canonical
  `cmm.cognitive.resources.ResourceProvenance` contract directly. `provenance`
  must be an exact `ResourceProvenance` instance; mappings, duck-typed
  lookalikes and serialized shapes are rejected and the contract deliberately
  exposes no `from_dict()` rehydration path. Derived `source_provenance_id`.
- `ReasoningAuthorityContext.authoritative_claim_ids` (field) replaced by
  `authoritative_claims: tuple[AuthoritativeSourceClaim, ...]`, with
  source-domain/purpose binding validation and duplicate-claim-id rejection.
  `authoritative_claim_ids` remains available as a derived read-only property,
  so a provenance-free authoritative claim is no longer constructible.
- `ReasoningRuleContext` authority semantics unchanged: optional
  `authority_context`, never serialized by `to_dict()`, explicitly rejected by
  `from_dict()`.

### 2.2 `cmm/domains/cognitive_integration.py`

- `build_reasoning_authority_context(..., authoritative_claims=...)`.
- Only claims that bind to the live `PermissionGateResult` are promoted:
  exact `AuthoritativeSourceClaim` type, matching source domain, matching
  canonical cross-domain purpose, and a requested resource id. Unbound,
  mapping-shaped, duck-typed or naked entries are never promoted and never
  raise. `DENY` / `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` still produce `None`.

### 2.3 `cmm/domains/neurodivergence/rules.py`

- `_trusted_health_authority(...)` now requires the requested claim to be
  present as a *provenance-bound* trusted claim bound to the Health source
  domain and purpose, and surfaces `source_provenance_id` and the authoritative
  claim projection in the rule-result `canonical_authority` metadata.
- Caller metadata (including canonical-looking provenance and
  authoritative-claim shapes) still has zero authority effect; `RULED_OUT`
  remains fail-closed.
