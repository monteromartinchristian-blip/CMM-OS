## 1. Purpose

Phase 10.53 adds the fourteenth first-party CMM OS Domain Pack:

```text
domain:neurodivergence
```

The pack specializes CMM OS for exploratory, longitudinal and
evidence-sensitive reasoning about neurodevelopmental questions.

Its core job is to help the user:

- understand patterns and possible associations;
- explore why a hypothesis may fit;
- explore why it may not fit;
- compare plausible explanations;
- organize developmental and longitudinal evidence;
- understand overlap among neurodevelopmental, emotional, medical, academic and
  contextual factors;
- prepare structured evidence for professional assessment;
- preserve the exact certainty/status of every material claim.

The pack is **not** a diagnostic engine.

It must be useful enough to reason openly with the user without becoming either:

1. an unsafe diagnosis generator; or
2. an over-defensive system that treats every exploratory question as something
   to refute.

The central design principle is:

```text
exploratory inference is allowed
diagnostic promotion is not
```

## 4. Domain relationship to Health and Mental Health

`domain:health`, `domain:mental-health`, and `domain:neurodivergence` are
independent sibling Domain Packs.

Neurodivergence does not inherit Health clinical authority.

Neurodivergence does not become a subdomain of Mental Health.

### 4.1 Health remains authoritative for

Health is authoritative for:

- documented diagnosis status;
- medication;
- treatment;
- medical tests;
- medical contraindications;
- clinical safety;
- medical records as medical records;
- clinician-authored clinical status where represented canonically.

Neurodivergence may organize and reason over purpose-authorized projections of
that material, but it must not overwrite Health authority.

### 4.2 Mental Health remains authoritative for

Mental Health is authoritative for its emotional and therapy specialization,
including:

- therapy continuity;
- emotional context;
- therapy-source provenance;
- emotional interpretations;
- ordinary emotional support;
- mental-health-specific safety boundaries.

Neurodivergence may consume purpose-minimized Mental Health context when the
current permission path permits it.

### 4.3 University remains authoritative for

University remains authoritative for:

- courses;
- exams;
- grades;
- academic deadlines;
- institutional constraints;
- academic records and planning context.

Neurodivergence may reason about functional academic impact without rewriting
University facts.

### 4.4 Relationships remains authoritative for

Relationships remains authoritative for relationship-specific events and
context.

Neurodivergence may reason about social patterns through a neurodevelopmental
lens without taking ownership of relationship facts.

## 6. Exploration-friendly reasoning

This design explicitly rejects a defensive interpretation of epistemic safety.

The system must be capable of genuinely exploring with the user.

### 6.1 Allowed exploratory behavior

The system may:

- notice patterns;
- notice associations;
- compare the user's described experiences with known conceptual patterns;
- generate working hypotheses;
- explain why something could fit a hypothesis;
- explain what does not fit or remains unclear;
- compare several hypotheses;
- discuss overlap;
- discuss possible co-occurrence;
- discuss alternative explanations;
- identify evidence that would increase or decrease plausibility;
- connect current observations with authorized longitudinal context;
- say that one explanation appears more plausible than another when the
  available evidence supports that comparative judgment;
- help formulate better questions for an assessment;
- prepare a structured summary of evidence.

Model inference is therefore allowed as **exploratory inference**.

### 6.2 Forbidden promotion

The system must not convert any of the following, by itself, into a confirmed
diagnosis:

- model inference;
- similarity to diagnostic criteria;
- an isolated trait;
- a screening score;
- self-report;
- retrospective recollection;
- a conversation pattern;
- academic difficulty;
- sensory difficulty;
- social difficulty;
- executive difficulty;
- emotional difficulty.

The forbidden transition is:

```text
exploratory/model inference
        ↓
confirmed clinical diagnosis
```

unless the confirmed clinical status is supplied through the appropriate
canonical Health-authority evidence.

### 6.3 Differential reasoning must be balanced, not adversarial

The pack must not behave like a prosecutor trying to disprove every hypothesis.

For an exploratory question such as:

```text
"Could this fit autism?"
```

a useful response may cover:

```text
why it could fit
what evidence supports that possibility
what is unclear
what may point elsewhere
what alternative or overlapping explanations exist
what evidence would clarify the picture
```

It does **not** need to provide a mechanically symmetrical list of reasons for
and against in every reply.

Negative evidence must be proportionate to its material relevance.

The system must not inject routine counterarguments solely to avoid appearing
supportive of a hypothesis.

### 6.4 No disclaimer spam

Ordinary exploratory conversation should not repeatedly collapse into:

```text
"I cannot diagnose; consult a professional."
```

The system may state the status boundary when materially relevant.

Professional assessment may be suggested as a useful source of clarification,
but that should not replace actual reasoning.

### 6.5 Working hypothesis

A **working hypothesis** is explicitly allowed.

A working hypothesis:

- may be used inside the current reasoning context;
- may guide evidence organization;
- may be compared against alternatives;
- may be presented to the user as a hypothesis;
- must preserve uncertainty;
- must not silently become a confirmed fact;
- must not silently become persistent memory;
- must not silently become Health clinical status.

## 7. Epistemic and certainty architecture

Phase 10.53 must not introduce a second Knowledge Model.

The canonical Phase 8 Cognitive Layer remains the source of truth for knowledge
kind, provenance, uncertainty and contradictions.

The required visible certainty hierarchy is:

```text
CONFIRMED
IN EVALUATION
HYPOTHESIS
NOT CONFIRMED / RULED OUT / INSUFFICIENTLY SUPPORTED
```

### 7.1 Semantic interpretation

#### CONFIRMED

`CONFIRMED` means that the relevant status has authoritative evidence.

For a clinical diagnosis, Health authority is required.

A model conclusion cannot create `CONFIRMED`.

#### IN EVALUATION

`IN EVALUATION` means there is real evidence that an evaluation or assessment
process is underway.

It is not a weaker synonym for diagnosis.

It is also not a model inference.

#### HYPOTHESIS

`HYPOTHESIS` is the normal state for:

- exploratory model inference;
- plausible user-generated explanation;
- a differential possibility;
- an interpretation that has not been confirmed by the authoritative source.

Hypotheses are useful and expected.

#### NOT CONFIRMED / RULED OUT / INSUFFICIENTLY SUPPORTED

These meanings must remain distinct.

`NOT CONFIRMED` does not necessarily mean `RULED OUT`.

`INSUFFICIENTLY SUPPORTED` does not necessarily mean contradicted.

`RULED OUT` should require appropriate source authority if represented as a
clinical assessment outcome.

### 7.2 Mapping to canonical Phase 8 semantics

The implementation should reuse existing canonical `KnowledgeKind`,
provenance, uncertainty and contradiction contracts.

No new global epistemic enum is required.

If a typed Neurodivergence-local status label is needed for presentation or
rule evaluation, it must be:

- pack-local;
- derived from canonical evidence;
- non-authoritative by itself;
- non-persistent as a second truth;
- unable to upgrade canonical knowledge;
- unable to grant clinical status;
- serializable only as part of pack-local output/metadata where appropriate.

A local label must never become a second knowledge store.

## 11. Minimum rule set

The pack must define a minimum rule set covering these responsibilities:

```text
neurodivergence.certainty_state_preservation

neurodivergence.source_authority

neurodivergence.contradiction_preservation
neurodivergence.differential_explanations
neurodivergence.overlap_reasoning
neurodivergence.purpose_minimized_cross_domain
neurodivergence.global_attribution_guard
neurodivergence.sensitive_label_persistence
```

The exact class names are implementation detail.

The canonical rule IDs and semantics are not.

### 11.1 Certainty-state preservation

The rule must reject silent promotion such as:

```text
HYPOTHESIS -> CONFIRMED
SCREENING -> CONFIRMED DIAGNOSIS
MODEL INTERPRETATION -> CLINICAL FACT
```

### 11.2 Clinical-status authority

A clinical diagnosis status supplied by canonical Health evidence cannot be
overwritten by Neurodivergence reasoning.

### 11.3 Source authority

Source-domain ownership is attribute- and purpose-sensitive.

A University fact remains a University fact.

A therapy-context fact remains Mental Health context.

A Health clinical fact remains Health-authoritative.

### 11.4 Developmental temporality

Current and historical observations must remain distinguishable.

The pack must not assume that:

```text
historical trait == current impairment
current difficulty == lifelong developmental pattern
retrospective report == contemporaneous observation
```

### 11.5 Observation/report separation

Direct observation, retrospective self-report and third-party report must not
be collapsed into one evidence type.

### 11.6 Screening/diagnosis separation

Screening may contribute evidence.

It must not itself become diagnosis.

### 11.7 Trait/function separation

A trait does not automatically imply clinically significant impairment.

Functional relevance may be analyzed separately.

### 11.8 Longitudinal corroboration

The pack may compare evidence across periods and sources.

Lack of longitudinal corroboration may reduce confidence but must not be treated
as automatic disproof.

### 11.9 Contradiction preservation

Competing evidence must remain visible.

The pack must not erase evidence that weakens a favored hypothesis.

### 11.10 Differential explanations

The pack should actively compare plausible explanations when useful.

The goal is understanding, not forced diagnostic exclusion.

### 11.11 Overlap reasoning

The system may reason about overlap among:

- TDAH;
- TEA/autism;
- AACC;
- TERIA/ARFID;
- dysgraphia;
- emotional/mental-health factors;
- medical factors;
- academic/environmental factors;
- relationship/social context.

Overlap is not automatic co-diagnosis.

### 11.12 Purpose-minimized cross-domain imports

Only fields required for the current task may enter from supporting domains.

A structurally valid transfer is not sufficient authority.

Current permission authority must gate transfer admission.

### 11.13 Global-attribution guard

The system must not explain every difficulty through neurodivergence merely
because the domain is active.

This guard must not suppress reasonable exploratory associations.

### 11.14 Sensitive-label persistence

Useful during-session hypotheses must not silently become stable memory.

Persistence remains proposal-first and approval-bound.

## 14. Permissions

The pack must use the canonical `DomainPermissionPolicy`,
`DomainPermissionResolver` and `DomainPermissionGate`.

The policy must distinguish, through existing capabilities/contracts:

- read;
- infer/reason;
- persist;
- cross-domain transfer;
- export;
- communicate;
- external mutation.

### 14.1 Exploration is not persistence

Permission to discuss or infer a working hypothesis does not imply permission
to persist it.

### 14.2 Read is not transfer

Permission to read a Health report does not imply permission to transfer it into
another domain context.

### 14.3 Provider availability is not permission

A provider/model route being technically available does not authorize remote
processing of `SENSITIVE` Neurodivergence material.

### 14.4 No autonomous external communication

Assessment material, diagnostic hypotheses and sensitive developmental history
must not be autonomously communicated to a third party.

## 15. Canonical approval semantics

Phase 10.53 must incorporate the Phase 10.52 V4 lesson from the beginning.

A `CrossDomainContextTransfer` is evidence/projection structure.

It is **not** permission authority by itself.

The connected path must be:

```text
current DomainPermissionResolver
        ↓
DomainPermissionGate
        ↓
effective current authority
        ↓
if APPROVAL_REQUIRED:
    canonical approval lifecycle
        ↓
APPROVAL_CONSUMED
        ↓
only then admit matching transfer
        ↓
Neurodivergence reasoning
```

Required fail-closed cases:

```text
DENY + matching transfer -> transfer not admitted

APPROVAL_REQUIRED
+ matching transfer
+ no consumed approval
-> transfer not admitted

approval for resource A
+ transfer for resource B
-> B not admitted

approval for actor/session/purpose A
+ mismatched current request
-> transfer not admitted
```

No Neurodivergence-specific permission or approval engine may be introduced.

## 28. Security model

This pack is security-sensitive because it handles:

- sensitive clinical/developmental context;
- diagnostic hypotheses;
- school/assessment records;
- psychometric results;
- cross-domain transfers;
- persistent personal labels.

Threats include:

### 28.1 Diagnostic promotion

```text
model inference -> clinical fact
```

Control:

```text
certainty-state preservation + Health authority
```

### 28.2 Transfer laundering

```text
valid CrossDomainContextTransfer
without current permission authority
```

Control:

```text
DomainPermissionResolver
+ DomainPermissionGate
+ canonical approval consumption
before transfer admission
```

### 28.3 Sensitive-label persistence

```text
useful working hypothesis
-> silent durable memory
```

Control:

```text
proposal-first memory
+ current permission
+ approval
+ preserved hypothesis status
```

### 28.4 Source-authority loss

```text
Health / Mental Health / University / Relationships fact
-> re-emitted as Neurodivergence-owned fact
```

Control:

```text
source-domain authority + provenance preservation
```

### 28.5 Over-attribution

```text
active Neurodivergence domain
-> every difficulty explained by neurodivergence
```

Control:

```text
global-attribution guard + differential reasoning
```

The control must not become blanket hypothesis suppression.

## 30. AT-DP-053 connected acceptance

`AT-DP-053` must use real canonical components or their official in-memory
implementations.

It must not be a collection of isolated mocks.

At minimum it must prove the following connected checkpoints.

### Checkpoint 1 — canonical registration

```text
domain:neurodivergence
```

registers through the canonical registry path.

### Checkpoint 2 — profile resolution

`NeurodivergenceProfile` resolves through the real profile registry/resolver.

### Checkpoint 3 — exploratory inference is allowed

A request to explore a plausible neurodevelopmental explanation must produce a
useful hypothesis-oriented reasoning result rather than being blocked merely
because no diagnosis is confirmed.

Required invariant:

```text
EXPLORATORY_MODEL_INFERENCE=ALLOWED
```

### Checkpoint 4 — exploratory inference is not diagnosis

The same exploratory result must remain a hypothesis/model interpretation.

Required:

```text
MODEL_INFERENCE_TO_CONFIRMED_DIAGNOSIS=BLOCKED
```

### Checkpoint 5 — certainty hierarchy

Confirmed, in-evaluation, hypothesis and unsupported/ruled-out semantics remain
distinguishable.

No silent upward transition is permitted.

### Checkpoint 6 — screening/self-report non-promotion

Screening and self-report can contribute evidence but cannot independently
create confirmed diagnosis.

### Checkpoint 7 — developmental chronology

Historical and current evidence remains temporally distinguishable.

### Checkpoint 8 — observer/source separation

Direct observation, retrospective self-report and third-party report remain
separate.

### Checkpoint 9 — balanced differential reasoning

The pack can explain:

```text
why hypothesis A may fit
why it may not
what overlaps with B/C
what evidence would clarify
```

without being required to adversarially refute A.

### Checkpoint 10 — Health authority

A Health-authoritative clinical status cannot be overwritten by
Neurodivergence reasoning.

Medication/treatment changes remain blocked.

### Checkpoint 11 — DENY + matching transfer

Using the real current permission system:

```text
DENY
+ structurally valid matching transfer
-> transfer not admitted
```

### Checkpoint 12 — unconsumed approval

```text
APPROVAL_REQUIRED
+ matching transfer
+ no consumed approval
-> transfer not admitted
```

### Checkpoint 13 — consumed approval

Using:

```text
DomainPermissionResolver
DomainPermissionGate
ApprovalService
official in-memory approval repository
```

prove:

```text
APPROVAL_REQUIRED
-> canonical approval created/granted
-> APPROVAL_CONSUMED
-> exact matching transfer admitted
```

### Checkpoint 14 — authority tuple binding

Approval for one resource/actor/session/purpose cannot authorize a mismatched
transfer.

### Checkpoint 15 — Mental Health source authority

Imported emotional/therapy context remains Mental Health-authoritative.

### Checkpoint 16 — University source authority

Imported academic context remains University-authoritative.

### Checkpoint 17 — Relationships source authority

Imported relationship context remains Relationships-authoritative.

### Checkpoint 18 — cross-domain minimization

Only exact relevant authorized fields are included.

No accepted transfer for field A may launder field B.

### Checkpoint 19 — SENSITIVE privacy

The canonical Phase 10.50 privacy path yields an effective policy at least as
restrictive as the Neurodivergence `SENSITIVE` floor.

### Checkpoint 20 — proposal-first memory

A working hypothesis is not silently persisted.

A permitted memory update is represented as a canonical proposal with preserved
hypothesis/certainty semantics.

### Checkpoint 21 — canonical Knowledge Package

The real `KnowledgePackageBuilder` and Phase 10.49 schema path produce/validate
the Neurodivergence package.

No second builder exists.

### Checkpoint 22 — presentation and trace

Presentation preserves certainty/source distinctions.

Domain Trace remains reference-only and passes the canonical validator.

### Checkpoint 23 — operations/workflows

Eight operation definitions and eight workflows are reachable through canonical
registries/contracts and do not introduce a new runtime/engine.

### Checkpoint 24 — benchmarks/quality

The pack declares valid Phase 10.47 benchmark and Phase 10.48 quality metadata
with no evaluator/runtime ownership.

### Checkpoint 25 — anti-fragmentation

No prohibited parallel owner exists.

### Checkpoint 26 — first-party inventory transition

Current canonical inventory becomes:

```text
FIRST_PARTY_DOMAIN_PACKS=14
CURRENT_DEFERRED_DOMAIN_PACKS=0
```

while historical inventory evidence remains intact.

### Checkpoint 27 — prior-domain regressions

Health, Mental Health, University, Relationships, General and earlier packs keep
their existing authority and behavior.
