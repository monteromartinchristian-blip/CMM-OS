## 6. Required MAJOR-03 design

Fix the first-party schemas, not the canonical builder or integration request.

### Life Plan

Remove:

```text

```

Life Plan may still constrain `active_goals` when present if the existing Phase 10.49 contract gives that policy meaningful semantics.

Do not add:

```text
KnowledgePackageRequest.active_goals
DomainCognitiveIntegrationRequest.active_goals
```

### Concerns

Remove:

```text
missing_information.required_non_empty=True
```

Concerns may preserve uncertainty, contradiction visibility, and missing-information awareness when present.

Do not add `DomainCognitiveIntegrationRequest.missing_information` solely to satisfy the schema.

### General principle

A first-party schema may impose `required_non_empty=True` only when the required field is reachable through the canonical package-construction path used by that Domain.

A field existing on the final dataclass is not sufficient evidence of reachability.
