## 1. Purpose

Phase 10.52 adds the first post-core specialized Domain Pack:
`domain:mental-health`.

The phase specializes the already-closed Domain Intelligence substrate for:

- ordinary emotional conversation and support;
- therapy continuity;
- pre-therapy preparation;
- post-therapy review;
- therapy-session and therapy-transcript analysis;
- longitudinal emotional context;
- emotionally relevant decision support;
- separation of fact, observation, interpretation, hypothesis, fear, intuition,
  preference, decision and uncertainty using existing epistemic contracts;
- non-pathologizing loop / rumination detection;
- proportionate safety escalation;
- purpose-minimized cross-domain coordination.

This phase must not create a second mental-health runtime, memory system,
knowledge graph, safety engine, planner, workflow engine, validation system,
privacy engine, permission engine, trace system, model router or other parallel
authority owner.

The Domain Pack is a specialization layer over existing canonical contracts.

## 4. Design point

### DP-052 — Mental Health Domain Specialization

CMM OS shall support a first-party `domain:mental-health` Domain Pack that
specializes ordinary emotional conversation, therapy continuity, therapy
analysis, longitudinal emotional context and emotionally relevant decision
support while:

- avoiding default medicalization or diagnostic framing;
- preserving Health as the authority owner for documented clinical facts,
  diagnosis status, treatment, medication and medical safety;
- preserving existing Cognitive, Memory, Knowledge, Privacy, Permissions,
  Trace, Validation, Workflow, Planner and Agent Runtime authorities;
- preserving provenance and epistemic status;
- requiring explicit authority for persistence, transfer, export,
  communication or external side effects;
- failing closed on authority downgrades;
- using purpose-minimized cross-domain projections;
- introducing no parallel infrastructure;
- remaining independent of Phase 10.53 Neurodivergence.

## 8. Authority boundaries

### 8.1 Health owns clinical medical authority

`domain:health` remains authoritative for:

- documented diagnoses;
- diagnosis status;
- treatment plans;
- medication;
- medication changes;
- contraindications;
- clinical medical records;
- physical medical risk;
- psychiatric treatment facts when they are clinical facts;
- medical safety decisions.

Mental Health may consume authorized projections of those facts but may not
overwrite, reinterpret as fact, or become the canonical clinical owner.

Example:

```text
"A psychiatrist changed my medication and emotionally I feel different."
```

Expected composition:

```text
primary = domain:health
supporting = domain:mental-health
```

unless another existing resolver rule provides an even more appropriate
canonical composition.

### 8.2 Mental Health owns emotional / therapeutic specialization

`domain:mental-health` owns specialization for:

- emotional context;
- ordinary emotional conversation;
- therapy continuity;
- therapy preparation;
- therapy review;
- therapy-transcript interpretation;
- longitudinal emotional patterns;
- emotionally relevant decision support;
- non-diagnostic emotional loop detection;
- reflective distinctions between fact and interpretation.

Example:

```text
"Prepare tomorrow's session with my psychologist."
```

Expected resolution:

```text
primary = domain:mental-health
```

with supporting Domains only when materially relevant and authorized.

### 8.3 Relationships owns relationship facts

Relationships remains authoritative for relationship-specific facts and
structured relationship context.

Mental Health may consume a minimal authorized projection for emotional
meaning but must not duplicate the Relationships Domain state.

### 8.4 Reflection owns general reflective structure

Reflection remains the general reflective Domain.

Mental Health may specialize emotional / therapeutic reflection when it is the
resolved primary or supporting Domain.

### 8.5 Concerns owns concern organization

Concern organization does not automatically imply mental-health authority.

An ordinary concern must not be reclassified as mental-health pathology merely
because it contains anxiety-like or emotionally loaded language.

### 8.6 General remains fallback

General remains the canonical fallback Domain.

### 8.7 Neurodivergence is deferred

Phase 10.53 remains `NOT_STARTED`.

Phase 10.52 may define an optional future relationship with
`domain:neurodivergence`, but must not:

- import a nonexistent Neurodivergence package;
- require its presence;
- register its contracts;
- implement any of its behavior;
- silently absorb its future responsibilities.

Absence of Phase 10.53 must degrade cleanly.

## 9. Mental Health semantic modes

The design recognizes four semantic modes. These are **not separate runtimes**,
**not separate Domain Packs**, and **not independent persistence systems**.

They are behavioral contexts represented using existing profile/rule/workflow
mechanisms.

### 9.1 Ordinary emotional conversation

For normal discussion of feelings, worries, relationships, personal meaning or
day-to-day emotional experiences.

Requirements:

- no default clinical presentation;
- no default diagnosis language;
- no severity scoring unless explicitly supported by an existing approved
  contract and appropriate authority;
- normal human conversational framing;
- preserve uncertainty;
- ask clarifying questions when ambiguity materially affects reasoning.

### 9.2 Therapeutic reflection

For:

- preparation for therapy;
- review after therapy;
- therapy notes;
- therapy transcripts;
- therapy continuity;
- identifying questions/topics to bring to a therapist.

Requirements:

- therapist/user/model speaker separation;
- provenance preservation;
- source fidelity;
- no fabricated therapist statements;
- no converting model interpretation into therapist advice;
- no invented diagnosis.

### 9.3 Clinical psychiatric information

When the task depends on documented clinical psychiatric facts such as
diagnosis, treatment or medication, Health owns primary clinical authority.

Mental Health may participate as supporting emotional specialization.

### 9.4 Material immediate-risk context

The Domain must represent proportionate escalation obligations through existing
canonical safety / permission / workflow / validation authorities.

It must not create a new crisis engine.

Ordinary distress, sadness, anxiety, anger, loneliness, grief, rumination or
emotional intensity does not by itself become an emergency.

## 10. Epistemic discipline

Mental Health is especially sensitive to accidental fact promotion.

The Domain must preserve existing canonical epistemic distinctions rather than
inventing a second claim ontology.

At minimum, reasoning and output must preserve distinctions analogous to:

```text
FACT
OBSERVATION
INTERPRETATION
HYPOTHESIS
FEAR
INTUITION
PREFERENCE
DECISION
UNCERTAINTY
```

when supported by the existing canonical epistemic model.

If exact enum/member names differ in production code, implementation must reuse
those canonical names. The phase must not add a parallel persistent taxonomy
solely to match this document.

Rules:

- interpretation must never silently become fact;
- fear must never silently become prediction;
- intuition must never silently become evidence;
- repeated thought must never silently become diagnosis;
- correlation must never silently become causation;
- a therapist statement must retain therapist provenance;
- a user statement must retain user provenance;
- model interpretation must remain model interpretation.

## 11. Longitudinal emotional context

The Domain may reason over longitudinal context only through existing canonical
memory / knowledge / temporal mechanisms.

It must not create:

- `MentalHealthStore`;
- `MentalHealthMemory`;
- `MentalHealthTimeline`;
- a second Knowledge Graph;
- a second temporal engine;
- a second contradiction engine.

Longitudinal reasoning may compare authorized canonical references across time,
while preserving:

- provenance;
- validity;
- confidence;
- epistemic type;
- privacy;
- permission;
- temporal semantics.

## 12. Persistence model

Talking about a sensitive inference does not authorize persistence.

Reading an existing memory does not authorize creating a new memory.

A model-generated interpretation does not become durable user truth
automatically.

Sensitive updates must follow the existing proposal-first canonical path.

The intended rule is:

```text
READ
!= INFER
!= PROPOSE_PERSISTENCE
!= PERSIST
!= TRANSFER
!= EXPORT
!= COMMUNICATE
!= EXTERNAL_MUTATION
```

No step grants the next step implicitly.

---

## 13. Privacy

Mental Health data is highly sensitive.

The Domain must use the canonical Phase 10.50 privacy system and must not
introduce another privacy policy engine.

The default should be restrictive and consistent with:

```text
SensitivityLevel.SENSITIVE
```

and an appropriate canonical privacy policy such as local-only or the existing
most-restrictive equivalent determined from the repository's current canonical
contracts.

The implementation must use the actual existing enum and policy members. It
must not invent `PrivacyPolicy.SENSITIVE`.

Remote processing, export, cache, disclosure or cross-domain transfer remain
subject to the existing canonical privacy and approval mechanisms.

---

## 14. Permissions

The Domain must use the existing:

```text
DomainPermissionPolicy
DomainPermissionRegistry
DomainPermissionResolver
DomainPermissionGate
```

or their current canonical equivalents discovered at implementation time.

Mental Health permission policy must be restrictive.

Requirements include:

- read authority is not write authority;
- analysis authority is not persistence authority;
- memory proposal authority is not direct mutation authority;
- export requires explicit authority;
- external communication requires explicit authority;
- cross-domain access is purpose-minimized;
- current authority must be revalidated;
- stale authority must never be reused;
- most-restrictive composition wins.

No `allow_cross_domain` privacy shortcut may be introduced.

## 15. Cross-domain projection rules

Cross-domain context is imported only when:

1. relevant to the current objective;
2. authorized by canonical permissions;
3. allowed by canonical privacy;
4. compatible with current resolution/composition;
5. minimized to the purpose;
6. provenance-preserving.

Examples:

### Mental Health + Relationships

Allowed:

- minimal relationship context needed to understand emotional meaning.

Not allowed:

- wholesale duplication of Relationships memory.

### Mental Health + Health

Allowed:

- authorized clinical facts needed to contextualize emotional effects.

Not allowed:

- Mental Health rewriting diagnosis, treatment or medication truth.

### Mental Health + Reflection

Allowed:

- reflective structure.

Not allowed:

- creating a second reflection engine.

### Mental Health + Concerns

Allowed:

- current relevant concerns.

Not allowed:

- treating every concern as a mental-health symptom.

### Mental Health + Neurodivergence

Deferred until Phase 10.53.

Any future reference must be optional and absent-safe.

2. do not medicalize by default;
3. preserve epistemic uncertainty;
4. preserve speaker/source provenance;

## 19. Operations

The initial canonical operation catalog should include, subject to validation
against existing operation-contract conventions:

```text
mental_health.review_emotional_context
mental_health.prepare_therapy_session
mental_health.review_therapy_session
mental_health.analyze_therapy_transcript
mental_health.compare_emotional_periods
mental_health.map_fact_interpretation_uncertainty
mental_health.review_emotional_decision
mental_health.propose_memory_update
```

Operation IDs may be adjusted only to match an established naming convention
discovered in existing first-party packs.

### 19.1 review_emotional_context

Produces structured emotional reflection over authorized context.

Must not diagnose.

### 19.2 prepare_therapy_session

Produces potential agenda items, themes, questions and unresolved items for a
future therapy session.

Must not fabricate therapist guidance.

### 19.3 review_therapy_session

Reviews user-provided session notes or references.

Must preserve speaker/source provenance.

### 19.4 analyze_therapy_transcript

Analyzes an authorized transcript.

Must preserve:

- source identity;
- speaker identity;
- quoted-vs-inferred distinction;
- uncertainty.

### 19.5 compare_emotional_periods

Compares authorized longitudinal periods using canonical temporal / memory
references.

Must not create an independent timeline store.

### 19.6 map_fact_interpretation_uncertainty

Separates known facts, observations, interpretations, fears, hypotheses and
uncertainty using canonical epistemic contracts.

### 19.7 review_emotional_decision

Supports decisions with meaningful emotional content while preserving
preference, uncertainty and contextual trade-offs.

### 19.8 propose_memory_update

Creates a proposal/binding through the existing canonical memory proposal path.

It must never imply direct persistence.

## 29. Safety design

Mental Health does not own a new crisis system.

Safety behavior must be expressed through existing:

- Domain rules;
- permissions;
- validation;
- workflow gates;
- Agent Runtime authority;
- canonical safety / policy mechanisms already present in the repository.

### 29.1 Ordinary distress

Sadness, anxiety, grief, anger, loneliness, uncertainty, fear or rumination do
not automatically trigger emergency framing.

### 29.2 Material safety conditions

If the existing canonical system resolves a material immediate-risk condition,
the Domain may:

- change presentation posture;
- require additional confirmation;
- route through an existing safety workflow;
- restrict unsafe operations;
- expose appropriate next-step guidance.

It must not invent authority that the shared system does not provide.

### 29.3 Prohibited safety behavior

- autonomous external contact;
- hidden emergency dispatch;
- fabricated diagnosis;
- fabricated risk certainty;
- ungrounded severity scoring;
- silent transfer of sensitive data;
- direct treatment changes.

## 30. Therapy transcript requirements

Therapy transcripts are particularly sensitive.

Any transcript operation must preserve:

```text
source
speaker
timestamp / ordering when available
verbatim-vs-summary distinction
model interpretation distinction
privacy
permission
provenance
```

The model must never attribute its own interpretation to a therapist.

The model must never upgrade an ambiguous utterance into documented clinical
fact.

If transcript provenance is insufficient for a requested high-confidence
conclusion, the result must preserve uncertainty.

---

## 31. Therapy continuity

Therapy continuity may use authorized canonical longitudinal references.

It should support:

- previous session themes;
- unresolved questions;
- repeated concerns;
- explicit user goals;
- user-reported therapist suggestions;
- changes over time;
- preparation for the next session.

It must not:

- fabricate therapist memory;
- create an independent therapist-record store;
- replace professional clinical records;
- present model interpretations as clinician statements.

---

## 32. Loop / rumination detection

The Domain may identify repeated reasoning or emotional loops when evidence
supports that characterization.

Requirements:

- non-diagnostic;
- uncertainty-preserving;
- no disorder inference solely from repetition;
- no severity score invented from conversation frequency;
- distinguish repeated topic from repeated unresolved reasoning.

The output should be useful without pathologizing.

---

- intuitions;

evidence.

## 63. Approved design summary

Phase 10.52 creates exactly one new first-party sibling Domain Pack:

```text
domain:mental-health
```

It provides:

- non-clinical emotional support specialization;
- therapy continuity;
- therapy preparation/review;
- transcript analysis with provenance;
- longitudinal emotional reasoning;
- emotionally relevant decision support;
- epistemic separation;
- proposal-first sensitive memory updates;
- restrictive privacy;
- purpose-minimized cross-domain coordination;
- proportionate safety behavior;
- real connected DP/AT acceptance.

```text
MENTAL_HEALTH_SPECIALIZES
CANONICAL_INFRASTRUCTURE_OWNS
MOST_RESTRICTIVE_AUTHORITY_WINS
SENSITIVE_CONTEXT_IS_MINIMIZED
CLINICAL_AUTHORITY_STAYS_WITH_HEALTH

PHASE10_53_REMAINS_DEFERRED
```
